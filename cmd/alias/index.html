alias

Create shorthand names for commands or command sequences.

⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Aliasing destructive commands (e.g. rm → rm -i) gives a false sense of safety — aliases do not apply in scripts or other shells.
What it does

alias lets you define custom shorthand for long or complex commands. Aliases defined in the terminal session are temporary — to make them permanent, add them to ~/.bashrc or ~/.bash_profile. alias without arguments lists all current aliases.

Examples
