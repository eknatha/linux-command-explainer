high riskActs on real Azure resources in the current subscription.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Check the subscription before every change (az account show).
  • `az group delete` removes all resources in the group.
  • Service principal secrets must not be committed.
Safer / dry-run alternatives
az account show -o tableConfirm the subscription
az group show -n <rg>Inspect before deleting
