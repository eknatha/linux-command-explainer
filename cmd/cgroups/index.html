cgroups (advanced)

Inspect and manage control groups that limit process resources.

medium riskIncorrect limits can OOM-kill processes or starve services.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Setting memory/CPU limits too low OOM-kills or throttles services.
  • Use `systemctl set-property` (or unit drop-ins) rather than writing to /sys/fs/cgroup directly so changes persist.
What it does

Cgroups (control groups) limit and account for CPU, memory, I/O, and network resources used by groups of processes. Containers use cgroups heavily. The systemd-cgls and systemd-cgtop tools visualize the cgroup hierarchy. Direct manipulation is via /sys/fs/cgroup/.

Pro tip

Use `systemctl set-property myservice.service CPUQuota=50%` and `MemoryLimit=1G` to apply resource limits to any systemd service without touching container runtimes.
