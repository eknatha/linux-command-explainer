cryptsetup

LUKS disk encryption

high risk

interactive breakdown — edit the command and press Explain
$ 
high riskWrong commands destroy the key slots or format the volume; losing the passphrase loses the data.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Back up the LUKS header immediately after luksFormat — header corruption means total data loss.
  • Keep at least two key slots/passphrases and store the recovery key safely.
  • luksFormat erases the target device’s data.
Safer / dry-run alternatives
cryptsetup luksHeaderBackup <dev> --header-backup-file hdr.imgBack up the header
cryptsetup luksDump <dev>Review key slots
What it does

Sets up, opens and manages LUKS encrypted volumes.

Common flags
FlagMeaning
luksFormatEncrypt a device (erases data)⚠ Destroys existing data.
openUnlock a volume
closeLock a volume

luksRemoveKeyRemove a passphrase
