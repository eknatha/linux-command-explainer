disown

Detach a job from the shell

low risk

interactive breakdown — edit the command and press Explain
$ 
low riskThe process keeps running after logout.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Disowned processes lose their terminal — their output is lost unless redirected.
What it does

Removes a job from the shell’s job table so it is not killed on hangup (-h keeps it in the table but ignores SIGHUP).

Common flags
FlagMeaning
-hDo not send SIGHUP on exit
-aAll jobs
Examples
