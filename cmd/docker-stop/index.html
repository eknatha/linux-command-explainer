docker stop

Stop containers

medium risk

interactive breakdown — edit the command and press Explain
$ 
medium riskStops services; sends SIGTERM then SIGKILL after the timeout.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • `docker stop $(docker ps -q)` stops every container on the host.
  • Databases may need a longer -t to flush data.
What it does

Gracefully stops containers; default timeout 10 s.

FlagMeaning
