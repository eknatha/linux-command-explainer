iconv

Convert text encodings

low risk

low riskOutput can silently lose characters if the target encoding cannot represent them.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • -c silently discards unconvertible characters.
  • Write to a new file; do not redirect onto the input.
What it does

Converts between character encodings such as ISO-8859-1 and UTF-8.

Common flags
FlagMeaning
-fSource encoding
-tTarget encoding
-cDrop characters that cannot be converted
Examples
iconv -f ISO-8859-1 -t UTF-8 in.txt > out.txtConvert to UTF-8 · explain & risk-check →
