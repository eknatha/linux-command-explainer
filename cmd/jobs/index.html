jobs

List background and stopped jobs in the current shell.

What it does

jobs lists processes running in the background (&) or stopped (Ctrl+Z) in the current shell session. Combined with fg, bg, disown, and kill %N, it provides job control without needing PIDs.

Common flags
FlagMeaning
-l --list-pidsShow PID alongside job info
-p --pidsShow only PIDs
Examples

Pro tip

Use `disown -h %1` after backgrounding a job with & to prevent it from receiving SIGHUP when your session closes — a lighter alternative to nohup for already-running processes.
