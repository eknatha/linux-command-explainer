killall

Kill processes by name

high risk

high riskSignals every process with a matching name — on Solaris-like systems killall kills ALL processes.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Kills every instance on the host, including processes from other users or containers (if you are root).
  • `killall` without a name on non-Linux Unixes terminates everything.
  • Use SIGTERM first; SIGKILL skips cleanup and can corrupt data.
Safer / dry-run alternatives
pgrep -a <name>Preview what would be killed
killall -i -TERM <name>Interactive, graceful
What it does

On Linux killall <name> sends a signal (TERM by default) to every process with that exact name.

Common flags
FlagMeaning
-9Force kill (SIGKILL)
-uOnly the given user’s processes
-iAsk before each kill
-wWait for processes to die
-eRequire exact match
