kubectl describe

What it does

kubectl describe provides human-readable detail about any K8s resource: events, conditions, container specs, volume mounts, and more. The Events section at the bottom is the first place to look when a pod won't start — it shows exactly what Kubernetes tried and what failed.

Pro tip

When a pod is stuck in Pending/CrashLoopBackOff, always check `kubectl describe pod` first — the Events section usually tells you exactly why (image pull error, insufficient resources, PVC not bound).
