lvextend

Extend a logical volume

medium risk

medium riskGrowing is generally safe; wrong args can exhaust the VG.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Check free VG space (vgs) first.
What it does

Adds space to an LV; use -r to grow the filesystem as well.

Common flags
FlagMeaning
-LNew size or +size
-rResize the filesystem too
-lExtents
Examples
