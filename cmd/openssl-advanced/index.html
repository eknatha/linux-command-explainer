openssl advanced (advanced)

Advanced OpenSSL: TLS debugging, certificate inspection, and PKI operations.

medium risk

medium riskGenerating weak keys or exposing private keys has security implications.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Self-signed certificates and -k in clients disable trust — use a proper CA for production.
  • Never paste private keys into tickets or chat tools.
  • Check expiry before renewals: openssl x509 -noout -enddate.
What it does

Beyond s_client, OpenSSL handles the full certificate lifecycle: inspecting cert details, verifying chains, checking expiry, generating CSRs, and testing specific TLS ciphers and protocol versions. Essential for TLS troubleshooting.

Examples
openssl s_client -connect host:443 -servername host 2>/dev/null | openssl x509 -noout -datesCheck TLS certificate expiry date · explain & risk-check →

Pro tip

Use `openssl s_client -connect host:443 2>/dev/null | openssl x509 -noout -enddate` in monitoring scripts to alert on certificate expiry — check daily and alert when < 30 days remain.
