reboot

Reboot the system

high risk

interactive breakdown — edit the command and press Explain
$ 
high riskDisconnects all users and stops all services.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Check who is logged in and what is running (who, systemctl list-jobs) before rebooting.
  • On remote hosts confirm sshd, networking and fstab are valid or you may lose access.
  • Schedule maintenance windows and drain nodes (kubectl drain) first.
Safer / dry-run alternatives
shutdown -r +10 "reboot for maintenance"Give users a warning window
shutdown -cCancel a scheduled shutdown
What it does

Restarts the machine via systemd. May be immediate.
