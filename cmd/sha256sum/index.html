sha256sum

Compute or verify SHA-256 checksums

low riskRead-only.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • A checksum only proves integrity relative to the checksum you compare against — fetch it from a trusted channel over HTTPS (or verify a GPG signature).
  • Do not use md5sum/sha1sum for security decisions.
What it does

Prints or verifies SHA-256 digests; use to confirm downloads and backups are intact.
