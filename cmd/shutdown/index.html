shutdown

Power off or reboot the system

high risk

interactive breakdown — edit the command and press Explain
$ 
high riskStops all services and may make remote machines unreachable.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • `shutdown -h now` on a remote host powers it off — you may not be able to turn it back on.
  • Notify users and drain workloads first.
Safer / dry-run alternatives
shutdown -r +10 "message"Schedule with a warning
shutdown -cCancel if it was a mistake
What it does

Schedules a halt, poweroff or reboot with a warning message.

Common flags
FlagMeaning
-hHalt / power off
-rReboot
