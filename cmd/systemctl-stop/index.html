systemctl stop

Stop a service

high risk

interactive breakdown — edit the command and press Explain
$ 
high riskStops the service immediately; dependants may fail; stopping sshd/network can lock you out.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Check what depends on the unit and whether you reach the host through it (sshd, NetworkManager, firewalld).
  • Production stops cause outages — drain from the load balancer first.
Safer / dry-run alternatives
systemctl list-dependencies --reverse <unit>See dependants
systemctl status <unit>Check current state
What it does

Stops a unit. Unlike restart, nothing brings it back.
