systemctl

Control the systemd init system and service manager.

medium risk

medium riskStarting, stopping, or disabling services directly affects system availability.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Stopping or disabling the wrong unit (sshd, networking, firewalld) can lock you out of a remote host.
  • `restart` drops connections; use `reload` where supported.
  • After editing unit files run `systemctl daemon-reload`, then check `systemctl status` and `journalctl -u <unit>`.
What it does

systemctl manages service lifecycle (start/stop/restart/reload), enables services to start at boot, and queries status with recent logs. Always verify service status after restart in automated scripts.

Pro tip

Use `systemctl list-units --type=service --state=failed` to see all failed services — great for post-reboot health checks.
