terraform destroy

Destroy all managed infrastructure

high risk

interactive breakdown — edit the command and press Explain
$ 
high riskDeletes every resource in the state — databases, networks, data.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Confirm workspace and backend first (terraform workspace show).
  • Resources with prevent_destroy or deletion protection are the last line of defence — do not remove them to “make it work”.
  • Back up data (snapshots) before destroying databases/volumes.
  • Never use -auto-approve outside disposable environments.
Safer / dry-run alternatives
terraform plan -destroy -out=destroy.tfplanReview the destroy plan
terraform workspace showConfirm the environment
terraform destroy -target=<resource>Narrow the blast radius
What it does

Plans and removes all resources managed by the current configuration/workspace.
