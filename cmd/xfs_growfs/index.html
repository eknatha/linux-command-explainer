xfs_growfs

Grow a mounted XFS filesystem

medium riskGrowing is generally safe, but XFS cannot be shrunk.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • XFS cannot be shrunk — plan sizes carefully.
  • Ensure the underlying LV/partition has really been extended.
What it does

Expands XFS to fill the underlying device.
