high riskInstalls/removes packages as root; removes can cascade.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Use --assumeno to preview.
  • Remove can uninstall dependent packages (e.g. sudo) — read the list.
  • Use `dnf history undo` to roll back mistakes.
Safer / dry-run alternatives
dnf upgrade --assumenoPreview
dnf history undo <id>Roll back
