nfs (advanced)

NFS client and export tools

medium risk

medium riskNetwork mounts can hang processes when the server is unavailable; exports can expose data.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Use hard,intr/soft carefully: soft can silently lose writes, hard can hang processes.
  • Restrict exports by host and use root_squash; avoid no_root_squash.
  • Mount with nosuid,nodev,noexec where possible.
What it does

Mount NFS shares with mount -t nfs and export with /etc/exports and exportfs.
