nmap

Network scanner — discover hosts and probe open ports on a network.

medium risk

medium riskPort scanning triggers IDS/IPS alerts and is illegal on networks you don't own.
⚠ Risks & cautions
  • Scan only systems you own or are authorised in writing to test.
  • Aggressive timing/scripts (-T4, -A, --script) can crash fragile devices or trigger IDS alerts.
  • Scanning from a cloud VM can violate the provider’s acceptable-use policy.
What it does

nmap probes hosts and ports to map network topology. Always obtain explicit written permission before scanning any network you don't own or operate.

Common flags
FlagMeaning
-sVDetect service versions on open ports
-OEnable OS detection
-pPorts to scan (-p 22,80,443 or -p 1-1024)
-snPing scan only — discover live hosts
--openShow only open ports
Examples

Pro tip

Use `-T4` for faster scanning on local networks. Use `-T2` on slow WAN to avoid triggering rate limits.
